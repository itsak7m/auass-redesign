Four membership levels

Join AUASS

AUASS brings together astronomy and space institutions and societies in the Arab world, Arab scientific institutions such as institutes and universities whose programmes include astronomy and space sciences, specialists in countries with no Arab astronomy or space society or institution, and any body approved by the Supreme Council under clear criteria.

Membership levels

Full member

  • Astronomy or space institutions and societies in the Arab world that founded the Union, and those that join later.
  • Specialists in countries with no Arab astronomy or space society or institution.
  • Any individual whose membership is approved by the Supreme Council.

Associate member

  • Accepted as an associate member for special reasons.

Observer member

  • The Council of Arab Economic Unity.
  • Organisations of the League of Arab States.
  • Related specialised Arab unions.
  • Any individual whose membership is approved by the Supreme Council.

Honorary member

  • Granted in recognition of distinguished service to the Union and the Arab world.

How to join

  1. 1

    Except for observer and honorary members, the body or individual applying submits a formal application, undertaking to abide by the Union's statutes and to pay membership fees and dues.

  2. 2

    Documents showing that the membership conditions are met are attached to the application.

  3. 3

    The joining fee is paid within six months of notification of acceptance, and annual dues are paid on time.

When does membership end?
  • Non-payment of dues for two consecutive years.
  • Resignation by the member.
  • A material breach of the Union's statutes.
  • Dissolution of the Union or a General Conference decision to end it.
  • Any other material reason the Supreme Council considers sufficient.

Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences

The Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences: an astronomy and space body headquartered in Amman since 1998, affiliated with the Council of Arab Economic Unity of the League of Arab States, and a member of the International Astronomical Union.
