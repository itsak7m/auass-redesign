Science news · 30 March 2023

Our planet is unique

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

The planet Earth is one of the very small planets compared with trillions of similar planets, or ones of different sizes and properties, scattered across the universe in general and the Milky Way galaxy in particular. God Almighty created the living being to live on its surface, honoured and favoured with the bliss of various goods and the living conditions God Almighty prepared for this being: "Say: Travel through the land and observe how He began creation. Then God will produce the final creation. Indeed God is over all things competent" (Al-Ankabut 20).

This planet was born, developed and formed with its various environments, from its atmosphere, seas and biosphere, and life began in it within this vast universe (the sky and the heavens) made up of nebulae, galaxies, stars, suns, planets, planetary moons, gases, dust and smoke, in addition to dark matter and dark energy, about whose secrets we know little, very little, within its diameter estimated at 90 billion light years (for someone living on Earth). In our universe there are billions of trillions of celestial bodies differing in size, shape, temperature, density and age, and everything it contains, that is, everything God Almighty created in it. "Indeed, We have adorned the nearest heaven with an adornment of stars" (As-Saffat 6).

The space between galaxies and between stars is filled with matter made up of gas, dust and smoke, as well as dark matter and dark energy, meaning that smoke along with gas and dust is available and spread between the various celestial bodies. We therefore find that the sky is woven with these materials, as God Almighty said in His noble book "By the heaven containing pathways" (Adh-Dhariyat 7). That is, the sky was woven with galaxies and stars and all the materials between them made up of gas, dust and smoke.

The Earth, with a diameter of about 12,756 km (that is, larger in size and mass than the small rocky planets of the solar system), has as its nearest celestial body the Moon, which lies 384,400 km from it and orbits the Earth every 29.5 days, which is called the synodic month. The Moon's diameter is about 3,476 km, about a quarter of the Earth's diameter, and light takes about 1.3 seconds to travel from the Moon to the Earth (the Moon's mass equals 1.2% of the Earth's mass and its volume equals 2% of the Earth's volume). The Earth orbits the Sun at an average distance of 150 million km, about 400 times the Moon's distance from the Earth, and the Sun's angular diameter is about 400 times larger than the Moon's; so we find that they have one apparent angular size, which is why at a total solar eclipse the Moon's apparent disc sometimes covers the Sun's disc completely.

"Do they not look into the realm of the heavens and the earth and everything that God has created" (Al-A'raf 185). We have spoken much before about the Earth, but here we will touch on some properties for the sake of comparison with the other planets and to show why God Almighty chose it, among the other planets, for humans, animals and plants to live on its surface.

"And on the earth are signs for those with certainty" (Adh-Dhariyat 20). The Earth is the third planet in distance from the Sun and the largest of the inner planets in size, as its diameter exceeds Venus's by a few hundred kilometres. It is the only planet known in the universe so far on which there is life, because it lies at a suitable distance from the Sun and because it has the water necessary for life, which covers most of its surface, meaning that water is everywhere, even in the bodies of humans, animals and plants: "And We made from water every living thing."

The Earth has a good atmosphere in which there are clouds, winds, lightning and thunder, in addition to some other weather phenomena. God Almighty says of thunder and lightning in His clear book: "It is He who shows you the lightning, [causing] fear and aspiration, and generates the heavy clouds. And the thunder exalts [God] with praise of Him — and the angels [as well] from fear of Him — and He sends thunderbolts and strikes therewith whom He wills while they dispute about God; and He is severe in assault" (Ar-Ra'd 12 and 13).

The Earth is also distinguished among the rocky planets by having a magnetosphere that protects its atmosphere from lethal and burning electromagnetic radiation as well as from solar wind particles. There are also various geological features on the Earth's surface, mainly volcanic lava, the movement of tectonic plates, and erosion by wind, water, ice and others, and impacts that generate craters (by small solar system bodies).

The Earth is highly geologically active at present, unlike the other planets, and all these features beneficial to life on its surface were created by God, glory be to Him, and not for the other planets. This is the important difference from the rest of the planets and other celestial bodies in the solar system and perhaps in the galaxy, the other galaxies and the universe, which is why we have called it the mother planet or the homeland.

* Director of the University of Sharjah, President of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences
