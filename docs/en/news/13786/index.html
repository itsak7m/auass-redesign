Science news · 30 March 2023

Land and sea

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

God Almighty created us on a small blue planet (as we see it from space), and through hundreds of blessed verses in the Noble Qur'an commanded us to contemplate this beautiful planet, its atmosphere, surface, biosphere, land, seas, waters and interior, its difference from the other planets and its place in the solar system and in the Milky Way galaxy, and how He made it a paradise in this world (the paradise of faith), or a homeland, a native land and a resting place standing in its own right: "And you will have on the earth a place of settlement and provision for a time" (Al-A'raf 24), so that His servant man may draw from these heavenly phenomena evidence of the precision of its maker and the greatness and creativity of its creator, majestic is He. "Indeed, in the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of the night and the day are signs for those of understanding" (Al Imran 190).

That is why the Earth was mentioned, with its attributes and the goods God Almighty gave us, in the Noble Qur'an, His book of miracles renewed in every time and place, and for every generation, through hundreds of noble verses (the word Earth was mentioned singly and together with its derivatives in the Noble Qur'an 461 times, and the word came to denote the whole Earth in some places and a part of it in others).

When God Almighty created the Earth 4,540 million years ago (with a total surface area of about 510 million square kilometres), He formed it of continents (land) and oceans (sea). If we calculate the areas of the continents (which represent the outer part of the Earth's crust) in terms of dry land on its surface, we find they represent about 29% of the globe, that is, about 149 million square kilometres, and if we calculate the areas of the oceans, or let us say the water bodies on it, we find them 71% of the Earth's surface, that is, about 361 million square kilometres. God, glory be to Him, distributed His goods, favours and the advantages useful to living beings, and everything He created, majestic is He, over the Earth's surface and everywhere.

In the Book of God, majestic is He, the word "land" (barr) is mentioned 13 times, at 29%, and the word "sea" (bahr) is mentioned 32 times, at 71% (glory be to God for this numerical harmony between the reality on the Earth's surface and what God Almighty mentioned in His Noble Book for the words land and sea). It should be known that the proportions of land and sea on our planet are subject to change over the ages and eras, before the appearance of life and after it, and before the appearance of man and after him.

Let us read some noble verses about land and sea:

"And He knows what is on the land and in the sea. And no leaf falls but that He knows it" (Al-An'am 59). "It is He who enables you to travel on land and sea" (Yunus 22), as well as other noble verses (Al-Isra 70, Al-An'am 97 and 63… etc.)

Glory be to God for His power in creating the planet Earth for humanity in this vast universe, for God Almighty spoke truly when He said: "Alif, Lam, Ra. [This is] a Book whose verses are perfected and then presented in detail from [one who is] Wise and Acquainted" (Hud 1), and likewise His words:

"We will show them Our signs in the horizons and within themselves until it becomes clear to them that it is the truth. But is it not sufficient concerning your Lord that He is, over all things, a Witness?" (Fussilat 53).

In addition to the above, God, glory be to Him, created for us all kinds of goods and favours on the Earth's surface and made it a dwelling and a settled place, and the only one to this day containing life. He provided us with good things so that the Earth would be for us a place of settlement and provision for a time, and made it a place rich in water resources, as water forms 71% of the planet's surface: "And We made from water every living thing. Then will they not believe?" (Al-Anbiya 30). The oceans hold 96.5% of the Earth's total water, and the remaining percentage is distributed among rivers and lakes, in addition to ice cover, glaciers, groundwater, water vapour in the atmosphere and soil moisture, as well as the water contained in the bodies of living beings (of course water is distributed over the Earth's surface surely as salt water and fresh water).

It should be known that the slightest change in the ratio of the land surface area to the area of seas and oceans, due to the periodic change in sea level, greatly affects the climate. It is very likely that this effect would occur at the level of the whole planet Earth.

However much science and technology develop, and however much discoveries and innovations are renewed, the Noble Qur'an will keep pace with these developments and new findings, even after tens, hundreds or thousands of successive generations and years.

Our planet Earth is the mother, it is the homeland, it is the paradise of this world and a precious gift from the gifts of God the Most High, the Almighty to humanity. Glory be to Him for His favours, which cannot be counted or enumerated, on the Earth as homeland, and glory be to Him for creating other bodies such as the Sun, the Moon and the solar system and making them all for the benefit of the Earth and humanity on its surface, and making them in balance and measure in all their motions around the Sun in the solar system, the Milky Way galaxy and the universe, as long as God Almighty wills. "And the heaven He raised and imposed the balance" (Ar-Rahman 7).

* Director of the University of Sharjah, President of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences
