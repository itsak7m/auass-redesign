AUASS news · 5 February 2023

Activities of the Syrian Astronomical Society in cooperation with AUASS in 2022

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an observing session of the Moon and the planets in Latakia on 16 January 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an activity for children on comets, meteorites and meteors in Latakia on 22 January 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held a lecture on astronomy in Arab-Islamic civilisation at the Arab Cultural Centre in Aleppo on 16 February 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an activity on telescopes and how to use them in Latakia on 24 February 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for women and girls, for the IAU Women and Girls in Astronomy project, at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 6 March 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held a lecture entitled "The new astrolabe" at the Arab Cultural Centre in Aleppo on 16 March 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held a session to sight the Ramadan crescent at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 1 April 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an activity for children explaining the planets of the solar system in Hama on 12 April 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an activity for children on the planets, with solar observing and a tour of the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 10 May 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held a public activity for the International Day of Light on the dangers of light pollution for astronomy, at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 16 May 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held a public observing night at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 9 June 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for students of the Othman bin Affan Sharia School at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 14 June 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held a public observing night at Tishreen Park, part of its activities at the flower show, in Damascus on 1 July 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for children of the Circassian Charitable Society at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 28 July 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held a special astronomy activity for students who passed their preparatory and secondary exams, at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 10 August 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for children at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 30 August 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for children at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 8 September 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for teenagers at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 11 September 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an observing activity for children for World Space Week 2022 and the IAU's 100 Hours of Astronomy, at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 4 October 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for teenagers entitled "The stars" at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 11 October 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for children entitled "The astronomer Ibn al-Shatir" at the reading club in Damascus on 26 November 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, with AUASS Vice President Dr. Mohammad Al-Osairi, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity introducing exoplanets at the Syrian Astronomical Observatory in Damascus on 28 November 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity for children in Masyaf on 16 December 2022.

In cooperation with AUASS, the Syrian Astronomical Society held an astronomy activity entitled "Yes to science, no to astrology" in Hama on 26 December 2022.
