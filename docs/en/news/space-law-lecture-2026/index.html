Space Law: Present and Future

A lecture by researcher Zaid Al-Dahamsheh, held on the AUASS platform, on the evolution, challenges and future of space law.

On the evening of 1 September 2026, the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences and the Regional Office of Astronomy for Development in the Arab World held a lecture on the AUASS platform titled "Space Law: Present and Future", given by Zaid Al-Dahamsheh, a researcher and writer on space law and a specialist in international relations.

The lecture covered the concept of space law, how it has developed and why it matters for regulating space activities. It also reviewed the main issues and challenges facing space legislation as the space sector grows quickly and the roles of states, institutions and the private sector expand.

The speaker also looked ahead to the future of space law and the need to develop legal and legislative frameworks that keep pace with the sector and support the peaceful and responsible use of space.

The lecture drew strong attendance and engagement; participants' questions and comments enriched the discussion of the legal and political aspects of space activities.

It is part of the series of scientific and educational activities AUASS organises to spread scientific and space culture and to give Arab researchers and specialists a platform to discuss current issues in space science, technology and policy.
