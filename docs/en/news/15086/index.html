AUASS news · 14 April 2024

AUASS receives Eid al-Fitr well-wishers

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

The Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences received well-wishers on the occasion of Eid al-Fitr with an astronomy evening held on Saturday, 13 April 2024, attended by the Union's Secretary General, Dr. Awni Mohammad Khasawneh, and many members and friends of the Union and young astronomy organisations.
Many members and friends of the Union came to its headquarters.

The meeting discussed the importance of cooperation between the various national institutions and bodies present and how to develop cooperation in space and astronomy science and start training projects and activities that serve society and the country. It focused on the role of young people in advancing astronomy and space technology in the Arab region and stressed the importance of horizontal cooperation in achieving results in this field. Prof. Ali Al-Taani, AUASS member and head of the physics department at Al-Balqa Applied University, spoke about the importance of space and astronomy science, while Prof. Ali Nouh Al-Qudah, AUASS member, spoke about recent achievements and the importance of remote sensing applications in our daily and working lives.
Eng. Heba Qadoumi, head of the IEEE branch, and her team gave a presentation on the main goals and activities of this young organisation, which runs science and space technology activities through global partnerships.
Eng. Mona Salim, head of AESS, and Eng. Ahmad Mubaideen gave a brief overview of the organisation's vision, goals and planned activities, expressing their wish to strengthen cooperation with the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences.

At the end of the rich discussion, letters of good intent were signed between the parties, paving the way for memoranda of understanding with IEEE Jordan Section SIGHT and AESS once administrative and legal procedures are complete, to strengthen cooperation in science in general and space technology in particular.
On this occasion the publication of the first edition of the book "The Solar System" by the Union's youngest member, student Emma Al-Jawarneh, was announced and celebrated, and the valuable book was discussed with the audience, along with how to support Emma's talents in this field.

This special astronomy evening combined the celebration of Eid al-Fitr and sharing love and good wishes with loved ones and friends with a chance to exchange ideas and cooperate to deepen our understanding of space and astronomy science and spread passion for it across the Arab region.
Eid Mubarak to all ❤️
