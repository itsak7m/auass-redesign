Science news · 8 June 2019

Astronomers' views on sighting the crescent matter and must not be ignored

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

Al-Riyadh newspaper:

Sheikh Al-Obaikan discusses the start of the month between naked-eye sighting and astronomical observatories:

Much debate and dispute has arisen over the validity of the sighting of the Shawwal crescent this year and the validity of the Muslims' breaking of the fast on Tuesday. I would like to explain my opinion on this important matter in application of the saying of the Prophet, peace be upon him: "Religion is sincere advice." We said: "For whom?" He said: "For God, for His Book, for His Messenger, and for the leaders of the Muslims and their common people." Narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim. So I say:

First: one must return to specialists in every branch of knowledge, as the Almighty said: "So ask the people of the message if you do not know."

Second: there is no doubt that the beginning of months relies on a true legal sighting, not an imaginary one, either with the naked eye or with observatories, according to the decision of the Council of Senior Scholars in 1403 AH, in application of the saying of the Prophet, peace be upon him: "Fast when you see it and break the fast when you see it… the hadith." Narrated by Muslim.

Third: the statements of the specialists in this field, namely astronomers, must not be ignored. That is why those who attended the Crescents Conference held in Jeddah, scholars and astronomers from a number of countries of the Islamic world, which was opened in the presence of His Eminence Sheikh Abdulaziz bin Baz, may God have mercy on him, unanimously agreed that the statements of astronomers must be relied on in negation but not in affirmation, and Sheikh Ibn Uthaymeen, may God have mercy on him, issued a fatwa to that effect, because affirmation varies by place and time. The meaning of accepting their word in negation is that if they determine that the Moon sets before the Sun, then no claim by witnesses that they saw it may ever be accepted, because it would be seeing the non-existent, which is impossible. But if they say that it sets after the Sun, then the genuine testimony of the crescent can be accepted, and the validity of this sighting is confirmed by the appearance of the crescent in the observatories, because determining the possibility of sighting by degrees involves differences depending on the clarity of the air, the strength of eyesight and the like, as mentioned by Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah.

It is not permissible to disparage astronomers and cast doubt on their ability on the basis of mere delusions and fancies

Fourth: the regulation for seeking the crescent sighting must be applied, which was issued by the noble royal order based on the aforementioned decision of the Council of Senior Scholars and the decision of the Shura Council. It included forming committees in which astronomers take part with observatories, along with the help of those known for sharp eyesight. This means combining observatories with naked-eye sighting. Otherwise, what is the use of the presence of astronomers with observatories that bring the distant hundreds of times closer than the human eye's sight, if the claim of a witness to have seen it is then accepted while it is not seen in the observatory? That is impossible by reason, by law and by sense. For testimony to be accepted it must be free of anything that disproves it by reason, sense and law. With the observatory, witnesses and astronomers together, error and delusion are avoided, since it is impossible for the naked eye to see what is not seen by the observatory in the same place and at the same time, because the observatory magnifies the crescent hundreds of times. For example, the witness Yunus, known for sharp eyesight, whose sighting was relied on in past years and who is a resident of Tabuk, told us that in one of the months he and another sharp-eyed witness stood trying to sight the crescent, with an astronomer and an observatory with them. The other witness claimed that he had seen the crescent and Yunus said he was wrong. The astronomer said: look at the observatory, which enlarged the size of the body that witness saw, and it was a small piece of cloud.

The testimonies of witnesses given in Sudair, Shaqra and Al-Ghat are subject to an old, primitive method of calculation

Fifth: I went out with His Excellency the former Minister of Justice, Dr. Abdullah Al al-Sheikh, His Eminence the former Undersecretary of the Ministry of Justice, Sheikh Abdullah Al-Yahya, and His Eminence Sheikh Mohammed Al-Babtain, who is concerned with the subject of crescents, and a team from King Abdulaziz City, headed by His Highness Prince Turki bin Saud bin Mohammed, Vice President of the City, with astronomers as members and with them an observatory. Two witnesses from Tabuk accompanied us, one named Yunus, who is the one mentioned above. We stood in one of the months of that year on a high spot in Dhahrat Laban in Riyadh. The astronomers had determined in their calculations that the Moon that night sets before the Sun. When the Sun set, His Excellency the Minister of Justice asked the two witnesses to try to sight the crescent, to confirm the correctness of what the astronomers were saying, and indeed that was done and they did not see the crescent. The astronomers told us that the crescent on the coming night would set after the Sun, so we went out the following night to sight the crescent. Only the Minister of Justice, the Undersecretary and the other witness stayed behind, due to circumstances, and the Minister asked me to accompany the committee. We stood at the same site and I asked that the witness Yunus move forward so as not to see the observatory, and try to sight the crescent, and that was indeed done. After effort he saw the crescent and tried to show it to us by pointing out its location. We saw it with difficulty after the darkness had increased slightly. Even so, the astronomer with him had a book giving the coordinates of the crescent's location in each month and its angle of tilt. The coordinates were entered right after sunset and the device moved automatically towards the crescent's location and we saw it on the screen, very clear and magnified. In this way, thanks be to God, the correct naked-eye sighting agreed with the observatory's sighting. No sensible person believes that a human can see with the naked eye what cannot possibly be seen through an observatory. The strange thing is that those who go out to Hawtat Sudair, from among the committee's members, astronomers and others, did not see the crescent through the observatory on the night that the witnesses claim they saw it. Indeed the astronomers asserted for some months in which sighting by the naked eye was affirmed that it set before the Sun. Would any sensible person accept such testimony, with these people making light of people's minds, especially in this age when air pollution from dust and the smoke of aeroplanes, factories and so on has become widespread? And if we think well of them, we say they saw another planet according to the astronomers' statement. Also, do all these witnesses enjoy extraordinary sharpness of sight? It is also strange and astonishing that when His Highness Prince Salman bin Abdulaziz, Governor of the Riyadh region, ordered a number of astronomers with their observatory to go out with the most prominent witnesses of Hawtat Sudair for six months to confirm his ability to see the crescent, this witness could not see the crescent even in one month, with the crescent on the horizon and appearing in the observatory, as one of the astronomers told me. This witness used to say that he does not rely on calculation or telescopes, according to what some sheikhs and committee members who meet him told me. Then an electronic newspaper before Ramadan quoted him as saying that since 1415 AH he has relied on calculation and telescopes. Look at this contradiction: if what he claims is true, that he relies on telescopes, modern telescopes belie his telescope, and if he relies on calculation, the astronomers, the people of calculation, contradict him. It is also strange that these witnesses do not wish astronomers to be present with them and try to keep away from them so that the facts do not become clear, unlike the witness Yunus and those with him, who welcome the presence of astronomers and observatories. Worse still, the Supreme Court's decision number (27/H), dated 29–30/8/1432 AH, in which the start of this year's month of Ramadan was affirmed and which was announced in the media, stated the following: "Indeed it was established by the testimony of a number of upright witnesses that the Moon set that night before sunset." So, you who are sensible, how can a human see the crescent in daytime before sunset? That is impossible even with observatories, and can only be known by calculation.

When the Supreme Court took over the crescents file, some witnesses changed the way they testify so that they would not be exposed

I have a remark on the Supreme Court's phrase "upright witnesses": who certified these witnesses as upright and how was the certification done? It is noticeable that the practice in the courts is to ask for the endorsement of witnesses by other witnesses whose own condition is not known, and who themselves need endorsement from other witnesses; if those are not known, their endorsement is requested, and so on, leading to infinite regress and circularity. What the scholars mentioned is that the endorsement of witnesses be sought from persons known to the judge for uprightness or famous among people for it. As for what the courts practise, accepting endorsement from someone whose condition the judge does not know and who is not famous among people for it, that does not accord with the texts of the law.

We were glad earlier when the Supreme Court rejected the testimony of some of these witnesses, according to what some of its members told me, then we see it, unfortunately, accepting them this year.

It is known that if the crescent remains after the Sun by one degree, which is four minutes, it cannot be seen. Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah said: "If it is at one degree, this is not seen." Majmu' Fatawa, vol. 25, p. 186.

He also said: "But sighting is not governed by fixed degrees, for it varies with sharpness and dullness of sight, with the height or lowness of the place from which the crescent is sought, and with the clarity or murkiness of the air.

Some people may see it at eight degrees, and another may not see it at twelve degrees." Al-Fatawa 25/207. So the least he mentioned, may God have mercy on him, is eight degrees, that is, sixteen minutes.

Sixth: the astronomers determined that the crescent sets in Riyadh with the Sun and remains in the Makkah region after the Sun for four minutes, that is, one degree, and they said sighting at this time is impossible. Indeed it was not seen in the observatories, neither in Al-Hada nor in Jeddah. Would any sensible person believe that a human sees the crescent at this time with the Sun's glow still lingering and the crescent's body so thin? Even if the witnesses claimed that the crescent stayed in Hawtat Sudair for four minutes, that is, one degree, that is impossible, as Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah mentioned, and greater than this is that the Supreme Court believes that they saw it before the Sun and that it set before it, as in the statement mentioned above.

Seventh: in recent years the witness Yunus has come to deny the testimony of the witnesses and affirms that he did not see the crescent they claimed to have seen, despite his strong and sharp eyesight.

Eighth: we can reach near certainty in determining the time of the start of the month for a long period whenever the astronomers decide, by precise calculations, that the Moon will remain after sunset for a period in which observatories and the naked eye can see it. Then, if sighting is attempted that night, it is very unlikely that it will not be seen. In this way the harm and dispute that has occurred can be avoided, and a Muslim can build on this report, which brings the likelihood of the legal sighting much closer for him, and arrange his airline bookings and accommodation, especially in the Hajj season, in a way that avoids harm to him, as happened several years ago when the Supreme Judicial Council in its permanent body made us fast only twenty-eight days and ordered us to make up a day, and as happened in the Hajj of a past year several years ago when the Council announced the day of standing at Arafat and days later announced a change of the day of Arafat to the day before, which caused confusion for many pilgrims in aeroplane bookings and harm to some Muslims in Europe in determining the time of slaughtering the sacrificial animals previously agreed with companies or institutions.

Ninth: what some have mentioned, that they saw the crescent on the night of Wednesday and it remained forty minutes, and on that basis concluded that the sighting of the crescent on the previous night is valid, is a mistake, because the specialists among the astronomers say that the duration of the crescent's stay is based on its birth: the longer the period between its birth and its setting after the Sun, the longer its stay and the larger its size.

Tenth: some scholars famous for issuing fatwas told me that he was driving his car along a road with one of the witnesses who usually testify to sighting the crescent, and there was a sign ahead of them. The sheikh asked the witness to read what was written on the sign and the witness could not read it, although the sheikh could read what was written on it clearly. Some told me that one of the witnesses claims to see what is behind clothes and incredible things, and this is a bar to accepting the testimony of these witnesses, and the legal rule is: "Impugning takes precedence over endorsing." The esteemed brother Dr. Khalid bin Saleh Al-Zaaq, the astronomy researcher and member of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences, told me words indicating that the witnesses' testimony is not valid, and wrote to me the following text: "On the day of sighting, the Sun set at the Hawtat Sudair stone observatory, in the layer of haze, 6 minutes before its true setting, and yet the testimony was affirmed." He said: "I have personal experience with the usual witnesses of more than twenty-five years, since the witness of Al-Hariq was raising the banner of testimony until the witnesses of Hawtat Sudair took the banner from him after he was exposed. Among these experiences is my experience with the sighting of the Shawwal crescent of this year, 1432 AH. On Monday 29 Ramadan 1432 AH I went to Hawtat Sudair and reached the court building at exactly 3 in the afternoon. I tried to find out the observation place in my own way but could not, and this is what I expected because their place is known to no one except the person concerned with the testimony, who determines a hidden place through telephone calls so that those he does not want do not go with him. I thought they would go to their stone observatory located between Jalajil and Al-Dahina, so I went to it and set up three telescopes, 12-inch, 10-inch and 8-inch, ready for observation, and waited for them. My expectation was disappointed as they did not come to this location, which is 900 m above sea level and is not a suitable place for observation because of the lights of the town of Al-Dahina in the south-west and the presence of a chain of winding heights on all sides. I reached the site at exactly 4:55 pm. The conditions were clear in the upper layers of the atmosphere and there was a haze belt that the eye could easily perceive stretching from north to south. The screens of the telescopes showed that the Sun sets at this location at exactly 6:26 pm and the Moon sets at 6:27 pm, meaning the Moon remains only one minute. After observing through the telescope lenses the following became clear:

  1. The Sun's rays began to turn yellow at exactly 5:55 pm
  2. The Sun's rays began to turn red at exactly 6:10 pm
  3. The Sun's lower ray dwindled at exactly 6:14 pm
  4. The Sun touched the dust layer at 6:17 pm
  5. Half of the Sun's disc vanished in the murk at 6:19 pm
  6. The entire Sun's disc vanished in the murk at 6:20:20 pm

"On noting the observation it became clear to me that the Sun disappeared from the telescope lenses 6 minutes before its true setting, in the murk layer. With the telescopes I could not see the Sun in all its majesty, splendour and strength, so how do these people see the crescent in the same area? Among these experiences is my experience with them on Wednesday 29 Ramadan 1431 AH, when I was able to go with the Sudair committee through the judge of the Kharj court, accompanied by the deputy head of Kharj Endowments. At that time some observations about the witnesses became clear to me and an impression formed in me, and I will not touch on any of that except regarding the day of sighting, corresponding to Wednesday 29 Ramadan 1431 AH, and I will highlight the most important of what their witness stated, with direction, noting that I documented the situation with pictures and handwritten notes of everything that came from them to reveal the truth of the matter. I set up my telescope, a self-adjusting device with a 10-inch lens. The observation site was wonderful and the conditions were clear in the upper layers of the atmosphere, but the layers touching the Earth's surface had clear haze, such that the Sun's lower rim began to vanish in this layer at exactly 6:06 pm, and after 65 seconds half the Sun had gone, and at exactly 6:09 the entire Sun's disc was immersed in the thick of the haze and the telescope, despite its great power, cannot perceive it. Then I was surprised that the sighter (A) claimed he still saw the Sun, and that he saw the crescent setting at exactly 6:07, with its position south of the sunset point by about 6 or 7 Sun-discs. I have some stops on this:

  1. The claim of brother (A) that he saw the Sun's lower disc touch the edge of the horizon at exactly 6:10:40 pm and that the Sun's disc was fully immersed at exactly 6:11:50 pm.

"If we do the calculation we find that (A) claims that he saw the crescent for one minute and ten seconds…

"A strong remark: the circumference of the Sun's disc, anywhere in the world, cannot take one minute and ten seconds under any circumstances. The Sun's disc at aphelion (when the Sun is at its farthest point from the Earth) takes a period of no less than two minutes at the equator, where the Sun sets vertically, so what of high latitudes such as the latitude of the Kingdom, which the Tropic of Cancer crosses, where the Sun does not set vertically but obliquely because of our tilt from the equator. I repeated the observation at the same place on Monday 4 Shawwal 1431 AH, when the conditions were very clear, and I saw the entire Sun's disc setting at the horizon, and the disc took two minutes and two seconds.

"I followed all the witnesses' testimonies in Sudair, Shaqra and Al-Ghat and realised that they follow an old calculation method for sighting the crescent, summarised as follows: they look at the time of the crescent's birth and the time of sunset from the astronomical calculations and work out the interval between them in degrees, so that every two hours is one degree, and they multiply the result by four, and the product is the crescent's stay. At the time of sighting they imagine the crescent will stay for this amount of time and they are made to think they saw it, and they spread that it stayed for the amount they work out in their minds. But the miscarriage of the Moon in some months of the year exposes the shallowness of this primitive process. These witnesses do not see the crescent at all, and if they were confident of their testimony they would give the media room to broadcast live the way they observe. Indeed we find that when the Supreme Court took over the crescents file they changed the way of testifying so that they would not be exposed: they began testifying to sighting crescents before sunset, and this is impossible by reason and science. They came forward with this testimony so as not to lose the Supreme Court, because it would not affirm their testimony when the Moon sets before the Sun as was done in the past, and not to lose people by not testifying, so they testify to sighting the crescent in an inverted way so that their testimonies in the past years, which contravene the precise cosmic law that God placed in this vast universe, are not exposed.

"I have a suggestion to the Supreme Court: any person who testifies to sighting the crescent should be given a realistic test, whereby he is brought, with legal, astronomical and psychological committees, in the middle of the lunar month to a place he does not know, shortly before moonrise in the east, and asked to show them the Moon as it rises. The Moon is full in the middle of the lunar month, and even so a person cannot see it as it rises until it climbs 5 degrees above the horizon and leaves the zone of dust and dirt, when it is at its farthest from the Sun, indeed the Sun is in the west and it is in the east. So how can these people's claim that they saw the crescent near the Sun, within the zone of dust and dirt and looking like a hair, be valid? Even the Sun, despite its brilliance and strength of light, we cannot see as it rises or sets on many days of the year with us because of the abundance of dust." End of his words.

The British Islamic Society also issued a challenge to anyone who sees the crescent on Tuesday night and put up a sum of money for whoever proves he saw it, as published in some newspapers.

Eleventh: it is not permissible to disparage astronomers and cast doubt on their ability on the basis of mere delusions and fancies, and it is not possible for them to agree on an error in this advanced science, which has proven the correctness of what they report regarding the duration of lunar and solar eclipses, the time of sunrise and sunset, the Sun's culmination at the zenith and everything related to pure astronomical science, even though they report this long before it happens. Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah determined that the calculator's report of a solar or lunar eclipse is neither knowledge of the unseen nor soothsaying and astrology. He said, may God have mercy on him: "As for knowledge of the custom in solar and lunar eclipses, only one who knows the calculation of their courses knows it, and the calculator's report of that is not a matter of knowledge of the unseen, nor of the rulings reported in which his falsehood exceeds his truth." Al-Fatawa (4/425), and Ibn Hubayra said something to the same effect.

So he confirms the correctness of their calculations, and it is not legally permissible for those who are not of this specialisation to accuse astronomers of lying and fabrication. It is regrettable that this has happened from someone affiliated with the fatwa institution; otherwise we would tell the rulers to close the astronomy departments in universities and King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology and do without observatories. Astronomers speak with knowledge, whereas witnesses offer mere sightings that could be of another celestial body, if we think well, especially given the state of some witnesses mentioned above. The scholars have determined that single-person testimony could be true or false. Is the definitive matter, built on modern science and precise calculation, to be abandoned and a speculative thing acted upon? This is what reason does not accept and the Islamic Sharia does not approve. The legal rule decrees that action is to be taken on certainty and doubt is not heeded, and this rule is taken from several texts, among them his saying, peace be upon him: "Let him cast away doubt and build on what he is certain of," narrated by Muslim, and his saying, peace be upon him: "He should not leave until he hears a sound or smells an odour," narrated by al-Bukhari and Muslim. Although this concerns prayer and purification, it includes acts of worship and other things, as indicated by his saying, peace be upon him: "Leave what makes you doubt for what does not make you doubt," narrated by al-Tirmidhi and al-Nasa'i, and al-Tirmidhi said: a good, sound hadith.

And let no pedant come and say that another legal rule says that the affirmer takes precedence over the denier. I say that what is meant by it is that the affirmer whose word is accepted is one whose affirmation is sound, not out of delusion and the like. The jurists mentioned that testimony of negation is accepted if it is bounded by time or place, and these observatories are at the same location to which the witnesses testify.

As for those who say that one must act on his saying, peace be upon him: "Fast when you see it and break the fast when you see it… the hadith," narrated by Muslim.

I say: no sensible person can understand that the Prophet, peace be upon him, commands us to fast or break the fast on an imaginary sighting, for whoever thinks this accuses the Islamic Sharia of building its affairs on delusions and fancies. I wonder at those who take modern science and advanced devices in all matters and act on them, then in the matter of crescent sighting cast doubt on it on the mere testimony of persons whose condition only God knows, and in any case it is speculative testimony that admits delusion.

Twelfth: I reassure Muslims of the validity of their fasting and breaking of the fast even if the witnesses erred, and the responsibility before God Almighty falls on the witnesses and the Supreme Court, based on his saying, peace be upon him: "The fast is the day you fast, the breaking of the fast is the day you break it, and the sacrifice is the day you sacrifice," narrated by al-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah, and in another narration: "Your fast is the day you fast, your fast-breaking is the day you break the fast, and your sacrifice is the day you sacrifice," adopted by Ibn Taymiyyah, and what the jurists determined, that if people err in standing at Arafat on the day of Arafat it suffices them and their Hajj is valid. Sheikh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah said: "If people stood at Arafat on the tenth day by mistake, the standing suffices them by agreement, and that day was the day of Arafat for them." Majmu' al-Fatawa, vol. 25, pp. 202–203.

In conclusion, I advise their eminences the President and members of the Supreme Court to fear God regarding the Muslims and the reputation of Islam, lest it be described as backward, rigid and contradicting scientific facts, and not to rely on the mere testimony of witnesses without verifying its validity by the easy scientific means I have mentioned above. The guardian of the affairs has placed his trust in them and made them responsible. I also advise those in charge of some channels not to interfere in what they do not do well of the science of calculation, astronomy and Sharia, for whoever speaks outside his field comes up with wonders. God is the grantor of success and the guide to the straight path, and may God's peace and blessings be upon our Prophet Muhammad, his family and his companions.

Adviser at the Royal Court
