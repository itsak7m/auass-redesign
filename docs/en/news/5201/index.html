AUASS news · 30 July 2019

UNOOSA Director Simonetta Di Pippo sends a letter of thanks to the Jordanian government through the Director of the Royal Jordanian Geographic Centre

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

Ms. Simonetta Di Pippo, Director of the Office for Outer Space Affairs, sent a letter of thanks to Jordan in an official letter addressed to Brigadier Dr. Eng. Awni Mohammad Khasawneh, Director General of the Royal Jordanian Geographic Centre and of the Regional Centre, thanking and praising the efforts made to ensure the success of the workshop "Global Partnership in Space Exploration and Innovation". The letter read as follows:

United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs

17 April 2019

Dear Brigadier Awni Mohammad Khasawneh,

Subject: United Nations/Jordan Workshop on "Global Partnership in Space Exploration and Innovation", 25–28 March 2019, Amman, Jordan

As Director of the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs, allow me to express my sincere gratitude to you and your team for your excellent support in delivering the recent United Nations/Jordan Workshop on "Global Partnership in Space Exploration and Innovation", held at the UN-affiliated Regional Centre for Space Science and Technology Education for Western Asia in Amman.

It was a pleasure to have the opportunity to experience the exemplary work of the Regional Centre, and the professionalism of your team must also be acknowledged. Through you, I express my sincere thanks for ensuring that my time in Jordan was productive, and I would particularly like to mention the efforts of Mr. Taha Masarweh and the leadership team, who worked diligently and generously to support my parallel institutional engagements throughout the mission.

The workshop brought participants from all over the world together in Amman to discuss the future of space exploration and innovation, and it could not have been such a remarkable success without the commitment and support of our Jordanian partners. Allow me once again to thank you personally for the role you played in making the workshop productive for all participants, through your long working hours and continuous efforts, especially in planning and coordinating the participants' contributions, which were sincerely appreciated.

The United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs looks forward to strengthening its cooperation with Jordan going forward, as we work together to bring the benefits of space science and technology to everyone, everywhere.

Simonetta Di Pippo

Director, United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs
