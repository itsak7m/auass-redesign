Science news · 22 June 2020

30 days until the Hope probe launches to Mars from Japan

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

Source: Dubai – Ahmed Yahya Date: 16 June 2020

The countdown to the launch of the "Hope Probe" to Mars continues, with 30 days remaining until this historic mission begins from the space centre on Tanegashima island in Japan, with 15 July set as the target date for the probe's launch.

According to the plan, the probe will set out on its mission to Mars at 5:51:27 am Japan time ("00:51:27" after midnight UAE time) on Wednesday 15 July, from Tanegashima Space Center using the Mitsubishi Heavy Industries "MHI H2A" launch vehicle. The Hope Probe is expected to reach Mars's orbit in February 2021, coinciding with the UAE's Golden Jubilee celebrations and 50 years since the declaration of the Union in 1971.

The "launch window" for this historic space mission runs from 15 July until 13 August. This date is subject to precise scientific calculations relating to the movement of the orbits of both the Earth and Mars, ensuring the probe reaches its planned orbit around Mars in the shortest possible time and with the least possible energy, and the "launch window" period extends over several days in anticipation of weather conditions and orbital motion.

83 hours

The operations of the probe's journey from Dubai to Japan exceeded 83 hours by land, air and sea, carrying out the timeline approved for the Emirates Mars Mission, despite the challenges caused by the spread of the novel coronavirus. Mitsubishi Heavy Industries was chosen to launch the probe because of its outstanding performance and distinguished reputation in space technology circles around the world, and its record of one of the highest success rates in launching spacecraft and satellites globally.

A group of young Emirati personnel are currently present to lead preparations and supervise every aspect of preparing the probe to set out on its historic space mission.

The "Hope Probe", the first Arab project to explore Mars, carries a message of hope for all the peoples of the region to revive the history rich in Arab and Islamic achievements in science, while embodying the UAE's ambition and its leadership's constant pursuit of challenging the impossible and overcoming it and entrenching this approach as a firm value in the country's identity and its people's culture, and it is also an Emirati contribution to shaping and making a promising future for humanity.
