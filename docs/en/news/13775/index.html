Al-Sa'im magazine – Paradise on Earth

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

Dr. Hamid Majool Al-Naimiy *

In our articles during the blessed month of Ramadan we will focus on the Earth as a homeland (as a mother), the most beautiful thing God created for humanity and living beings. We will review the properties and phenomena of the globe, its surroundings and its relation to celestial phenomena, and the verses of the Holy Qur'an that point to God's creation and supreme greatness, especially those concerning the Earth and its geological and physical components, its seas and its atmosphere; its place in the universe and its fitness for life on its surface; its unique difference from the planets of our solar system and the billions or trillions of other planets in the universe; and how its surface, its atmosphere with its layers and its magnetic field were created as a shield and refuge for living beings, a protective garment against harm and cosmic rays, including electromagnetic radiation and the solar wind, and a shield against fragments of comets, asteroids, meteors, meteorites and other natural and artificial objects that enter the atmosphere. And how God created in this envelope different kinds of shade to protect us from the Sun's heat and wind and from the harm of most kinds of electromagnetic radiation, such as gamma rays and X-rays (of high energy), ultraviolet and infrared rays, and the Sun's scorching electrons and charges.

When the Earth is photographed by spacecraft cameras from several million kilometres away, it appears to astronauts as a very small blue dot floating in space, barely visible against the background of stars. Through various scientific, technological, Islamic and social studies up to the present day, and with the technology currently available, the Earth has been found to be the only planet we know today, among the billions or trillions of planets in the observable universe, that holds billions of living beings, including humans of every race.

This planet is our mother, our homeland and our cradle, which God created and laid out for His creatures, making it subservient so that we may walk freely on its slopes and eat of His provision. He made it a gathering place, a bed and a cradle, traced paths for us in it, sent down water upon it from the sky, and brought forth with it pairs of diverse plants.

"Have We not made the earth a receptacle for the living and the dead, and placed in it lofty mountains, and given you sweet water to drink? Woe that Day to the deniers" (Al-Mursalat 25–28). God made this planet a place of settlement for us, built a sky around it, formed us and perfected our forms, and provided us with good things, so that the Earth would be our dwelling and our provision for a time. He made for us shade from what He created, and shelters in the mountains, and garments that protect us from the heat and garments that protect us in battle, to complete His favour upon us so that we may live safely on the Earth's surface in happiness and peace.

God created this planet as a resting place for us and its mountains as pegs; He created us in pairs and made our sleep a rest. Through its rotation on its axis and its revolution around the Sun He created night and day, making the night a covering and the day for livelihood. He made the Sun a blazing lamp for us and sent down abundant water from the rain clouds to bring forth grain, plants and dense gardens. On the Earth He created neighbouring tracts, gardens of grapes, crops and palm trees, growing in clusters or singly, watered with the same water. All are blessings beyond counting.

On this Earth He created for us those we love and those we know; He made us rejoice and grieve, and live among thousands of religions, theories and ways of life. Among us are children, young people and the old, mothers and fathers, brothers and sisters, children with a future, inventive discoverers and foolish destroyers, great leaders and weak ones, heroes and cowards, those who love and those who do not, the peaceful and the destructive, the loyal and the treacherous, and so on.

This planet is the only one we know, because we live on its surface, and through it we came to know the observable universe and its planets, stars, galaxies and nebulae, as well as many other components related to the life of living beings, from the smallest particles (it is believed today that the centres of black holes are distinguished by this unique smallness) up to the largest galaxies.

The largest galaxies have been observed at a distance of about 13.5 billion light years. In the constellation Lynx lies a giant radio galaxy, Alcyoneus, discovered in 2022, which is 16.3 million light years long, making it the largest known galaxy of all (about 154 times longer than our galaxy, with up to 400 billion stars and a mass of about 2,400 billion solar masses).

This galaxy was discovered in July 2022 by the James Webb Space Telescope, which observed the oldest galaxy ever, the first time in human history that a celestial object has been observed at such a distance.

* Chancellor of the University of Sharjah – President of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences
