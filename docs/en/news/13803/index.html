The fate of the Earth in the near future (2)

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

The near future of the Earth, let us say up to thousands or tens of thousands of years, up to whatever God Almighty wills in determining the Earth's fate, by its death and revival or its end entirely along with humanity.

God Almighty said: "He brings the living out of the dead and brings the dead out of the living and brings to life the earth after its lifelessness. And thus will you be brought out" (Ar-Rum 19). For this period of time there are many possibilities depending on several subjects, among them continuous technological progress without stopping, which we hope will be used properly to serve humanity in order to continue developing education and learning and health and improving human, animal and plant life and a comfortable living without notable wars and fighting, and attention to climate and weather changes in favour of a sustainable environment in a way that protects the environment, by thinking of protecting the Earth from environmental and cosmic harm and from space rocks and debris, and thus increasing the lifespan of humanity and of the mother Earth and reducing the harm humans do to the Earth and its contents, including natural disasters such as earthquakes, volcanoes, floods and climate change, or space and cosmic events such as the impact of some comets or asteroids and meteoroids.. etc. The events that affect and harm the Earth and its inhabitants in this period are many, among them the following:

– The global warming phenomenon and climate change because of the illegal consumption and production of energy resources harmful to the Earth's environment, the most important being the heat-trapping gases that cause global warming. This phenomenon is one of the main factors that lead to a continuing and severe oxygen shortage and an increase in carbon dioxide as a result of the release of hydrogen sulphide, deadly to all forms of life; as that may lead to the extinction of more than 95% of marine creatures and more than 60% of humans.

Let us take the blessed and important efforts the UAE is making on climate change, which was clear in the speech of His Highness the President of the State delivered at the opening of the 27th session of the Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (COP27), held in the city of Sharm El-Sheikh in Egypt last November, and His Highness's historic directive to host the 28th session (COP28) in the UAE next November.

– Excessive population growth, as the Earth's population approaches 8 billion people. It is known that overcrowding plays an important role in determining the health state of planet Earth, and that most of the problems humans suffer from now and in future are overpopulation, and it is expected to reach 9 billion people in 2030. This will certainly affect natural resources and biodiversity and increase health deterioration, although global space agencies and space institutions are trying to find solutions to that by searching for a planet near the Earth on whose surface humans can live. Perhaps an Earth-like planet to live on will be discovered, or space settlements built in orbits around the Earth, and this may ease the steady and excessive population growth on the Earth's surface.

– Wars are among the most dangerous problems facing humanity, as their causes are either natural and industrial resources, or ambition, or greed, or occupation, or revenge. The extinction of humanity may come through a third world war that could be nuclear, unconventional and destructive, God forbid, because of advanced and developed technologies including various uses of artificial intelligence applications, virtual reality, blockchain, big data, robotics technology, drones and cyber weapons.

– The impact of a comet, asteroid or large space body on the Earth. Today many institutions and scientists are thinking about this possibility and trying to find solutions to avoid a celestial body striking the Earth, by directing a space rocket at this body (which could be loaded with a nuclear bomb), or a laser beam or a solar net, to destroy this body or change its course if it is heading for the Earth. There is research through a specialised global organisation to track near-Earth asteroids or objects, which sweeps the sky to detect threats from comets and asteroids that may approach the Earth or collide with it. Consequently, the end or destruction of the Earth may be due to a celestial body striking the Earth, especially large ones (and the possibilities exist and are present), because the solar system contains billions of rocks and various celestial bodies such as the moons of most planets, meteoroids, comets and small asteroids found in the asteroid belt or in the Kuiper belt or the Oort cloud… etc. Many of these meteoroids, space rocks and materials fall on the planets including the Earth, to form meteors and meteorites.

Some global space institutions are trying to save the Earth from any asteroid or comet strike that may collide with the Earth, that is, to prevent natural space rocks from colliding with the Earth, especially the destructive ones.

* Director of the University of Sharjah, President of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences
