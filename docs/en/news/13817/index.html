Science news · 17 April 2023

The fate of the Earth in the very distant future

Translated from the Arabic original. اقرأ النص الأصلي بالعربية

We have a number of possibilities for the Earth's fate and end after millions or perhaps billions of years:

1 – Since the Earth and the planets of the solar system are tied to the Sun and orbit it, is the explosion of the Sun and its turning into a red giant after billions of years, then into a white dwarf and its death (its eventually turning into a black dwarf), the end of the Earth and the planets of the solar system? That is, does the death and collapse of the Sun lead to the collapse of the solar system including the Earth? That is, is this phenomenon the Day of Judgement?

According to astrophysical and space calculations, only five billion years remain of the Sun's life until its life cycle ends. After the above period the Sun becomes a giant star and its material (after its explosion) will cover the planets near to the Earth's orbit, and afterwards it dies and turns into a white dwarf the size of the Earth, then turns into a small dead black ball.

That is, after a series of physical processes inside the Sun's centre that lead to the end of hydrogen fusion and its turning into other elements such as helium, then carbon, silicon and oxygen… then to heavy elements such as iron (after a long period of time reaching thousands of millions of years), then the Sun explodes and turns into a red giant, while its centre (core) shrinks in on itself (because of the gravitational forces of the heavy elements formed in the core after helium burning), that is, the Sun is wound up on itself: "When the sun is wound up" (At-Takwir 1). The same processes happen to all stars (the helium in the core runs out and nuclear fusion stops, so the star's outer envelope, made of gas, dust clouds and smoke, explodes: "Then watch for the Day when the sky will bring a visible smoke" (Ad-Dukhan 10). So the collapse begins when the hydrogen fusing in the Sun's core runs out, and this disturbs the balance between the outward forces produced by nuclear fusion and the inward pull of gravity, and instead the star moves internally towards fusing helium with heavier and heavier elements.

When helium fuses, the Sun can swell to more than 400 times its size, and the outer layers cool and turn to become a red giant, and consequently it could swallow the planets Mercury and Venus and perhaps the Moon (that is, after its explosion it will cover the planets near it as far as the Earth's orbit). Here could be the end of the Earth, depending on how close the Earth is to the Sun, which could turn the Earth into a scorched, lifeless rock, as the oceans boil and evaporate and the atmosphere is stripped from it. Also friction between the Earth's outer layers and the Sun could slow the Earth's orbit, and this could therefore cause our planet to spiral slowly towards the Sun to be gradually destroyed.

Even stars go through the same stages depending on their mass: some turn into a white dwarf while others of the large-mass stars explode as supernovae and then turn into neutron stars or black holes (dead stars with very high gravitational forces from which even light cannot escape): "And when the stars fall, dispersing" (At-Takwir 2)

2 – Many scientists believe that the forces of attraction on Earth (tides) may change in the future, and also believe that the Earth's motion and nature will change over millions of years and destroy life on it (is it the end of the Earth and those on it? But the possibility exists (whatever the outcome, all is by God Almighty's command)). Or referring to some geophysical studies that the Earth's core will cool and this will eliminate the Earth's magnetic field, which will leave the field open for the solar wind to enter the Earth's envelope and bombard its surface with charged electric particles coming from the Sun and kill living things on it.

3 – It could be the collision of our galaxy with the galaxy nearest to us, the Andromeda galaxy, 2.5 million light years from us. Scientists expect that our galaxy will collide in the distant future with the Andromeda galaxy (each of these galaxies contains a black hole, and because of the gravity of these two holes the two galaxies may collide to form together one very massive black hole, and as a result the life of our solar system including the Earth will end).

4 – The end of the Earth, the planets, the galaxy and the universe could be when the universe returns (reverts) to what it was before (the universe contracts again, crunches together and becomes a joined entity anew). We know physically and astronomically that the universe is currently expanding, and the speed of this expansion is increasing in direct proportion to the distance between any two galaxies. According to one hypothesis, if the end of the universe comes with its density increasing to become much greater than the critical density, the universe's expansion will stop and it will contract again (the Big Crunch phase) and become as it was before the Big Bang.

"The Day when We will fold the heaven like the folding of a [written] scroll for the records. As We began the first creation, We will repeat it. [That is] a promise binding upon Us. Indeed, We will do it" (Al-Anbiya 104). Indeed God is Knowing of all things.

* Director of the University of Sharjah, President of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences
