The Arab Conferences for Astronomy and Space Sciences

The 9th Arab Conference on Space and Astronomy Sciences

The Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences
in cooperation with the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research, the Ministry of Science and Technology and the National Commission for Education, Science and Culture,
with the participation of the Islamic Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (ISESCO),
on the occasion of the International Year of Astronomy,
and under the patronage of the Vice-President of the Republic of Sudan, Mr. Ali Osman Mohamed Taha,

holds the 9th Arab Conference on Space and Astronomy Sciences under the theme:
Astronomy, space science and technology in the service of Arab and Islamic societies
17–19 November 2009 (Khartoum, Sudan)

Aims of the conference

  1. To follow the achievements and scientific contributions of scientists and researchers in astronomy, space and atmospheric sciences, in the Arab world and abroad, continuing the series of Arab conferences in these fields.
  2. To strengthen scientific and cultural ties among Arab scientists, researchers and enthusiasts in astronomy and space, develop joint research and projects, and open more channels of interaction among them in the Arab world and internationally.
  3. To join the international scientific community in celebrating the International Year of Astronomy (IYA2009), discussing research and taking part in astronomy outreach among the Arab Union's members.
  4. To use this Arab scientific gathering to hold the 7th meeting of the General Assembly of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences, to discuss the scientific, administrative and financial reports on the Union's activities since its founding in August 1998, and its current and future plans.

Conference themes

  1. Astronomy and space science in the modern Arab world:
    a – achievements of young Arab astronomers and space enthusiasts
    b – teaching astronomy and space science (schools and universities)
    c – activities and future plans of Arab astronomy and space institutes and centres
    d – scientific research in physics, astronomy and space science
  2. Arab and Muslim contributions to the cosmic sciences, and to astronomy in particular.
  3. Atmospheric and space sciences and their applications.
  4. Astronomy and astrophysics:
    a – promoting cosmic knowledge and public awareness of astronomy and space science
    b – ethics of space science, communications and their research
    c – administrative discussions of the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences

Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences

The Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences: an astronomy and space body headquartered in Amman since 1998, affiliated with the Council of Arab Economic Unity of the League of Arab States, and a member of the International Astronomical Union.
