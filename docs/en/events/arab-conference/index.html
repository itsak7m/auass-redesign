The Arab Conference for Astronomy and Space Sciences

The Arab Conference for Astronomy and Space Sciences – Introduction

The Arab Conference for Astronomy and Space Sciences is one of the most important conferences held by the Arab Union once every two years. These conferences played a major role in finding and building a database of space and astronomers throughout the Arab world and abroad.

The research papers presented at the conference by Arab researchers and their friends from other countries were numerous and varied, from astrophysics and star physics to cosmology, space geology, planetary science and solar activity and many research topics that are among the important research areas in enriching The Arab Library and helps Arab researchers in astronomy and space sciences.

The Twelfth Arab Conference in Astronomy and Space Sciences

The Twelfth Arab Conference on Space Sciences and Astronomy and the Seventh Islamic Astronomical Conference were organized by the AUASS, the Royal Jordanian Geographical Center, the Regional Center for Space Science and Technology Education for West Asia – the United Nations, the Regional Office of the Astronomy Division in the Arab Countries, and the Jordanian Astronomical Society.

A very important topics were discussed: in theoretical and observational cosmology (cosmology), astrophysics, other life in the universe, astronomical instruments and observatories, solar activity and space weather, satellites and their applications, celestial mechanics, astrogeology, archaeological astronomy and the history of astronomy, And astronomical applications in Islamic law, and other topics.

The eleventh Arab Conference in Astronomy and Space Sciences:

The 11th Arab Conference in Space Sciences and Astronomy was held simultaneously with the 2nd International Conference in the History of Arabs and Muslims Science at the University of Sharjah from 08 – 11 December 2014. The conference was based on several scientific sessions and lectures on observatories and Arab astronomical devices and discussed topics of the universe and its components of celestial bodies, including stars, galaxies, the solar system, meteors, meteorites and meteor craters. Sessions were devoted to discussing the latest findings of scientists in technology Remote sensing, monitoring and monitoring of the Earth from outer space, the physics of the sun and solar activities and their impact on the atmosphere, and the Arab scientific heritage in astronomy and the atmosphere.

The 10th conference in astronomy and space sciences

The Oman Astronomical Society in the Sultanate of Oman hosted the 10th AUASS Conference, which was held in cooperation with the Ministry of Transport and Communications, the International Astronomical Union (IAU), the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA), the Scientific Research Council, the US Space Agency (NASA) and the European Space Agency (ESA) and the Islamic Inter-Network for Space Sciences and Technology in 5-8 February 2012 at Sultan Qaboos University in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. The conference was held under the slogan:

“The Role of Astronomy and Space Sciences in the Development of Contemporary Societies”

The conference was held under the patronage of His Excellency Dr. Yahya bin Mahfouz Al-Mandhari, Chairman of the State Council of the Sultanate of Oman, in the Great Hall of the Sultan Qaboos University Cultural Center.

The 9th Conference in Space and Astronomy Sciences

The 9th Arab Conference in Space and Astronomy was held under the slogan:

“Astronomy and Space Science and Technology at the Service of Arab and Islamic Societies”

In the Republic of Sudan, at the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research in Khartoum, Sudan, for the period 17-19 November 2009 in cooperation with the Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences, the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research and the Ministry of Science and Technology

The 8th Conference in Space and Astronomy Sciences

The AUASS held its 8th conference in the resort of Vincci in the city of Djerba, Tunisia, from 20-23 / March / 2007 which was organized by the AUASS and the Association of Youth and Science in Tunisia / Djerba branch and participation of the European Space Agency (ESA).

More than 50 research papers were presented in the conference’s within 12 scientific sessions, including the opening sessions.

The 7th conference in space and astronomy sciences

The 7th Arab Conference on Space and Astronomy was held under the slogan

“Physics, Astronomy and Space in the Service of Arab and Islamic Societies”

on the occasion of the century’s passage of Einstein’s theory.

120 researchers from 17 Arab countries participated in the conference, in addition to experts from Bulgaria, Russia, France and the United States of America.

5th Conference in Space and Astronomy Sciences

Representing His Highness Prince Faisal Bin Al-Hussein, Dr. Hisham Ghusayb, President of Princess Sumaya University for Technology, inaugurated on Monday, August 19, 2002 the 5th Arab Conference for Space and Astronomy under the slogan:

“Establishing an Arab Space Research Agency”

Which was organized by the AUASS and the Jordanian Astronomical Society in cooperation with the University Princess Sumaya for Technology, in the university building.

Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences

The Arab Union for Astronomy and Space Sciences: an astronomy and space body headquartered in Amman since 1998, affiliated with the Council of Arab Economic Unity of the League of Arab States, and a member of the International Astronomical Union.
