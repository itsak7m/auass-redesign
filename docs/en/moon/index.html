Arab astronomical heritage

Lunar craters named after Arab and Muslim scholars

Twenty-five craters on the Moon were named by the International Astronomical Union in honour of scholars of Arab and Islamic civilisation. Seven lie on the far side, never visible from Earth. Spin the Moon and pick a crater.

Drag to spin the Moon; tap a marker to open a crater

Pick a crater from the list or on the Moon to see where it is, who it honours and why.

Abulfeda أبو الفِدا

Latitude
13.87° S
Longitude
13.91° E
Diameter
62.2 km

Named after

Ismail Ibn Abū al-Fidā; Syrian geographer (1273-1331).

Prince of Hama, historian and geographer, author of Taqwim al-Buldan (A Sketch of the Countries).
